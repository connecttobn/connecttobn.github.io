Our Process

How It Works

A simple, transparent journey from your first idea to your finished home.

1

Consultation

We visit your site or connect virtually to understand your lifestyle, preferences, and budget.

2

Design

Our designers create detailed layouts, 3D renders, and material boards for your approval.

3

Execution

Our craftsmen bring the design to life with quality materials and regular site updates.

4

Handover

FAQ

Common Questions

How long does a typical project take?

A single-room project takes 4-6 weeks; a full home interior takes 8-12 weeks depending on scope.

Do you provide 3D designs?

Yes, our Signature and Luxe packages include photorealistic 3D renders and virtual walkthroughs.

What cities do you serve?

We serve Bangalore, Mumbai, Delhi, Gurgaon, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune, and Jaipur.

Is the consultation free?

Yes, your first consultation and estimate are completely free with no obligation.
